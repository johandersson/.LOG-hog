Security Without Complexity

.LOG-hog is built around a simple philosophy: sensitive notes should remain local and protected. The application combines AES-GCM encryption, PBKDF2 key derivation, encrypted backups, clipboard protection, secure password handling and tamper-evident mechanisms while preserving an effortless writing experience.

Unlike many productivity tools, .LOG-hog never requires an online account or external service. Your data stays on your machine, encrypted and under your control.
