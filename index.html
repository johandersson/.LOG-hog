.LOG-hog is a lightweight note-taking application inspired by the classic .LOG functionality in Windows Notepad. Open the application, start typing immediately, and save timestamped entries without interrupting your workflow. Whether you're keeping a journal, tracking incidents, documenting research, or writing development notes, .LOG-hog is designed to stay out of your way and let you focus on writing. Everything is stored locally in a single encrypted file under your control. No accounts, subscriptions, telemetry, cloud synchronization or vendor lock-in.

Built for Logging, Journaling and Everyday Notes

Most note-taking applications are built around synchronization and online services. .LOG-hog is built around writing. Launch the application, write an entry, press Save, and continue. Each entry is timestamped and added to your log, creating a chronological record that remains easy to browse, search and manage over time. Inspired by the simplicity of Notepad's .LOG workflow, .LOG-hog extends the concept with encryption, search, markdown rendering, backups and practical security features while keeping everything local.

Your Notes, Secure by Design

.LOG-hog uses AES-256-GCM encryption, PBKDF2 key derivation (600,000 iterations), encrypted backups, integrity checks, and tamper-evident protections to help keep your notes secure.

Security still depends on your password, so .LOG-hog includes a built-in generator for strong passwords and passphrases. Like any local application, it cannot protect against malware, keyloggers, or a compromised system.

Important

Use a trusted device and a strong, unique password. If you forget your password, your encrypted notes cannot be recovered.

Simple Notes. Local Storage. Open Source.

.LOG-hog is released under GPLv3 and is completely open source. It takes the familiar .LOG concept from Windows Notepad and modernizes it with encryption, search, backups, markdown rendering and practical local security. The result is a lightweight application, built in pure Java, no extra libs, focused on fast writing and local ownership rather than cloud services. Review the source code, build it yourself, contribute improvements, or simply enjoy a straightforward note-taking application that keeps your data under your control.
